# Map augments in a run: Long-Ranged Scanners label neighbouring beacons; Adv. FTL Navigation
# lets us jump back to beacons already visited.
ship 1
seed 3
at 1 do closeevents
at 1.5 do aug ADV_SCANNERS
at 1.5 do aug FTL_JUMPER
at 2 expect aug_ADV_SCANNERS == 1
at 2 do map
at 3 shot scanners
at 4 do jump near
at 8 do closeevents
at 9 do jump near
at 13 do closeevents
at 14 log state
at 14 expect jumpfar >= 1
at 14 do map
at 15 shot jumper
at 16 done
