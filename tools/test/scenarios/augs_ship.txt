# Ship augments in a one-off fight: Fire Suppression and Slug Repair Gel clear fires and breaches
# with nobody in the rooms (fires re-spread faster than its Crystal-speed suppression, as in FTL: it only keeps them
# from growing much; the count at 30 s varies with the random rolls, so the limit allows some spread), Zoltan bypass etc.
# are fitted and counted.
ship 1
enemy REBEL_FAT
enemyfire 0
seed 5
at 1 do aug FIRE_EXTINGUISHERS
at 1 do aug SLUG_GEL
at 1 do aug CREW_STIMS
at 2 expect aug_FIRE_EXTINGUISHERS == 1
at 2 expect aug_SLUG_GEL == 1
at 2 do fire 3
at 2 do breach 3
at 2.5 expect fires > 0
at 2.5 expect breaches > 0
at 3 shot burning
at 30 expect fires <= 6
at 30 expect breaches == 0
at 30 shot after
at 31 done
