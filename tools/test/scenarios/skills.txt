# shot at us is made to miss (do evade 1: a real dodge, through the game's own miss path); waiting
# for a natural one was luck (20% evasion, and the enemy can knock out the pilot or engines, or kill
# the crew manning them). skillmax_ is the best level reached, so a crew member lost after
# levelling doesn't fail it.

at 1 do select 1

at 30 expect skillmax_pilot >= 1
at 30 expect skillmax_engines >= 1
